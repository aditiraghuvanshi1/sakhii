PMDD Clinical Guidance (ACOG/DSM-5)

Premenstrual Dysphoric Disorder (PMDD) is a severe, neurobiological reaction to natural hormone fluctuations during the luteal phase (7–14 days before menstruation).

Key Diagnostic Criteria (Must clear post-period):

  • Marked mood swings, sudden sadness, or intense tearfulness
  • Persistent, severe irritability, anger, or interpersonal conflicts
  • Marked anxiety, tension, or feeling "on edge"
  • Loss of interest in daily activities, fatigue, or sleep changes

Evidence-Based Management Steps:

1. Cycle Logging: Keep daily mood logs for 2–3 consecutive cycles to confirm timing.

2. Lifestyle Support: Aerobic exercise, reduced caffeine/alcohol, and Calcium/Vitamin B6 supplementation under supervision.

3. Medical Options: Consult your doctor regarding SSRIs or specific oral contraceptives approved for PMDD.

Red Flag Medical Alert

If you or someone you are assisting experiences any of the following symptoms, do not wait. Seek immediate hospital emergency care:

🚨 BP ≥ 140/90 with severe headache or vision loss (Pregnancy)

🚨 Sudden severe abdominal pain or heavy soak-through bleeding

🚨 Thoughts of self-harm or hurting the baby (Postpartum Crisis)

🚨 Chest pain, difficulty breathing, or sudden one-sided swelling

Call Emergency Helpline (108 / 112)
